Choose between minimum curvature gridding (with optional tension) and natural neighbor (Sibson) interpolation, using a dialog modelled on the native IDW interpolation algorithm. Both methods are vectorised numpy implementations; no extra Python packages are required (scipy is used for the Delaunay triangulation when present, otherwise the GEOS triangulation built into QGIS).
