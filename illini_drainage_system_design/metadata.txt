OutSlope now uses each segment's own length. The offset moves the outlet and the whole network follows it. New BURY_CHECK field flags segments whose grade is outside the slope limits.
    2.0.0 - Renamed to Illini Drainage System Design. Step H (Network Elevation Exports) no longer needs SAGA: end point elevations and terrain profiles are sampled from the DEM directly. Fixed missing Point_ID in End Point Elevations.
    1.0.0 - First standalone release. Includes the fix for per-segment drainage coefficient and spacing in pipe sizing.
