name=LiDAR Field DEM
qgisMinimumVersion=3.22
description=Bare-earth DEM for a field boundary from Illinois (ISGS ILHMP) or USGS 3DEP LiDAR.
about=Given a field boundary, finds the LiDAR covering it and builds a bare-earth DEM clipped to the field.
    ISGS (Illinois Height Modernization Program): queries the ISGS collection service, reads the collection tile index, and pulls only the needed LAS tiles out of the county-wide zip using HTTP range requests.
    USGS 3DEP (any U.S. state): streams the field's points from the public AWS Entwine Point Tile archive.
    Ground points are triangulated and rasterized with PDAL, then clipped to the field plus a 100 ft margin with GDAL.
    Requires the PDAL command-line program (for example from the OSGeo4W installer).
