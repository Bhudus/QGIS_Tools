about=Pick a location (or a point layer), choose GCMs and SSP scenarios, and the plugin extracts daily precipitation and maximum/minimum temperature for the grid cell, converts model calendars (no-leap, 360-day) to real dates, converts units, and writes ready-to-use DRAINMOD .RAI (hourly rainfall) and .TEM (daily Tmax/Tmin) files, identical to the output of DRAINMOD's weather utility, plus .wea text files, plus an optional Hargreaves PET (.PET) file and an ensemble summary. Sources: NASA NEX-GDDP-CMIP6 via the NCCS THREDDS NetCDF Subset Service (no download of full files), LOCA2 regional files from UCSD, local NetCDF files, or any THREDDS NCSS endpoint.
