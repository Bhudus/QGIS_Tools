description=Maps the drainage intensity of a designed tile network (in/day or mm/day) by the Hooghoudt or Kirkham equation, from each drain's depth, the local spacing between drains and the soil.
about=Takes the Drainage Network made by Illini Subsurface Drain Layout, Illini Subirrigation Layout or Illini Drainage System Design and maps, cell by cell, how fast its drains can remove water: by Hooghoudt's equation with the water table between the drains (van der Molen and Wesseling equivalent depth), or by Kirkham's equation for a ponded surface, as in DRAINMOD. The local spacing comes from the network itself (the distance from each drain to the drainage divide, or to the field edge), and the drain depth from its tile and ground elevations. The soil comes from a soil map such as the SSURGO or SoilGrids drainage soil maps (with the layer profile, the conductivities are worked out for each drain's own depth) or from uniform values. The raster is drawn in classes against a target drainage coefficient, and the log gives the field's mean and median intensity and the share below the target. Uses numpy and GDAL, which come with QGIS.
