Pick a soil map they made, enter a drain depth, drainage coefficient, mid-drain water table, impermeable layer depth or subirrigation water levels, and get a copy of the soil map with new drain and subirrigation spacings. Uses each soil's layer-by-layer Ksat (ksat_prof field), so no internet connection is needed. Run it again for each scenario.
