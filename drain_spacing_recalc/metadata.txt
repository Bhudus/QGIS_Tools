description=Recalculates drain and subirrigation spacings on a soil map from SSURGO or SoilGrids drainage soil map, for a new drain depth, coefficients and water table heights.
version=0.2.0
changelog=0.2.0 - Spacing ranges from low and high Ksat; drain footage and optional cost per soil; Field summary table with two design spacings for the field (equal drain length, and narrowest soil over a set share of the field).
    0.1.0 - First release: one scenario per run, US customary or metric units, Hooghoudt drain spacing with an optional mid-drain water table and inverse Hooghoudt subirrigation spacing, using each soil's ksat_prof field.
author=Richard Cooke
email=raccooke@gmail.com
about=Companion to SSURGO drainage soil map and SoilGrids drainage soil map. Pick a soil map they made, enter a drain depth, drainage coefficient, mid-drain water table, impermeable layer depth or subirrigation water levels, and get a copy of the soil map with new drain and subirrigation spacings, their range from low and high Ksat, and the drain footage and cost for each soil. A field summary table gives two design spacings for the whole field: the one that needs the same total drain length, and the narrowest spacing among soils covering a set share of the field. Uses each soil's layer-by-layer Ksat (ksat_prof field), so no internet connection is needed. Run it again for each scenario.
