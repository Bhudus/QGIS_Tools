about=For each field boundary polygon, builds the area inset by half of the tile (lateral) spacing plus a guide line on that edge, so laterals in neighboring fields end up one full spacing apart. Optionally fills the inset with parallel laterals at full spacing (set azimuth, per-field angle attribute, or parallel to the longest edge; aligned to the guide line or centered) and connects them to a supplied mains layer by splitting at crossings, extending straight to the main, or adding a shortest connector. A junctions layer marks tees and crosses. Spacing in feet or meters; geographic layers are processed in the local UTM zone. Available in the Processing Toolbox (Tile Drainage > Layout > Tile drainage layout), the Vector menu, and a toolbar button.
