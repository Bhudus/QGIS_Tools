name=Field Run-on
qgisMinimumVersion=3.34
description=Maps the run-on areas of a field: the land outside it whose surface water flows into it, from the field boundary and a DEM of its watershed. Vector and raster maps with their areas, the inlets where the water enters, and smoothed polygon boundaries.
about=For tile and surface drainage design: run-on is extra water the field's surface inlets, waterways and outlet have to carry. Routes water by D8 steepest descent over the Wang & Liu filled DEM (re-filled with a tiny gradient so flats and filled depressions drain to their spill point) and marks every cell outside the field whose flow path enters it. Splits the run-on by inlet (one area for each place water enters the field, with the inlet as a point and the acres it carries) or into connected areas. Outputs run-on polygons (optionally smoothed) with acres, hectares, share of the field and an open flag where the area reaches the edge of the DEM, a raster of each run-on cell's area, and the inlet points. Works with a StreamStats watershed and a LiDAR or 3DEP DEM; large DEMs are resampled for routing. Uses only QGIS, GDAL and numpy.
