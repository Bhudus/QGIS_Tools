name=Line Angle
qgisMinimumVersion=3.16
description=Returns the angle of a line segment in degrees, relative to the positive horizontal direction.
about=Activate the Line Angle tool and click on any line feature. The nearest segment is highlighted and its angle is shown in the message bar: measured counter-clockwise from the positive horizontal (east) axis in the map canvas CRS, in the range 0-360 degrees, following the segment's vertex order. The undirected orientation (0-180 degrees) is shown too, and the angle is copied to the clipboard.

    1.1.0 - Added Processing algorithm "Segment angles" that writes each segment's angle to a field.
