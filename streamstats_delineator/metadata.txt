about=Click on or near a stream; the point is snapped to the nearest StreamStats stream-grid cell and the watershed draining to it is delineated with the USGS StreamStats SS-Delineate service. NHDPlus streams inside the watershed are added from the USGS NLDI, and the watershed can be split into sub-basins at stream confluences from NHDPlus catchments, with downstream routing links. Results are added to scratch layers with drainage area, snap distance, region and HUC. Requires internet access; covers US states/regions where StreamStats delineation is available.
