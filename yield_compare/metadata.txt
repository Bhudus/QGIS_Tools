description=Statistically compare crop yields from two or more yield-monitor point layers, allowing for spatial autocorrelation, and test whether yield drops midway between tile drains.
about=Choose two or more (up to 8) point layers (e.g. yield-monitor shapefiles) with a yield column and, ideally, the harvest pass column. Reports descriptive, non-parametric and robust statistics, normality tests and exceedance values, and tests whether the fields have equal means, medians, variances and histograms, with the difference in mean yield and its 95% confidence interval; with three or more fields, tests across all fields plus pairwise comparisons adjusted for the number of pairs (Holm). Every test allows for spatial autocorrelation (a variogram-based correlation model with harvest-pass effects), so p-values are honest for yield-monitor data. Yield vs distance to tile: with a tile network (e.g. from Illini Subsurface Drain Layout), compares yield over the tile with yield midway between laterals, in distance bands across the drain spacing, and beyond the reach of the tile. Optional least-squares and reweighted regression of yield against another column, with slope tests. Colours both layers by yield. Includes two synthetic sample fields with tile networks (Load sample fields). Opens from Vector → Tile Drainage → Compare Field Yields. Started as a port of the University of Illinois VB6 Yield Statistics program.

    0.3.0 - Two synthetic sample fields with tile networks and a Load sample fields button. Drain spacing per field, read from DRAIN_SPACE of the laterals within each field. Layers on a local grid (engineering CRS or no CRS) are analysed in their own units.

Harvest pass column per field.
