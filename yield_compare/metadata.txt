description=Statistically compare crop yields from two yield-monitor point layers, allowing for spatial autocorrelation, and test whether yield drops midway between tile drains.
about=Choose two point layers (e.g. yield-monitor shapefiles) with a yield column and, ideally, the harvest pass column. Reports descriptive, non-parametric and robust statistics, normality tests and exceedance values, and tests whether the two have equal means, medians, variances and histograms, with the difference in mean yield and its 95% confidence interval. Every test allows for spatial autocorrelation (a variogram-based correlation model with harvest-pass effects), so p-values are honest for yield-monitor data. Yield vs distance to tile: with a tile network (e.g. from Illini Subsurface Drain Layout), compares yield over the tile with yield midway between laterals, in distance bands across the drain spacing, and beyond the reach of the tile. Optional least-squares and reweighted regression of yield against another column, with slope tests. Colours both layers by yield. Opens from Vector → Tile Drainage → Compare Field Yields. Started as a port of the University of Illinois VB6 Yield Statistics program.
