description=Statistically compare crop yields from two yield-monitor point layers: descriptive and robust statistics, normality tests, and tests of equal means, medians, variances, histograms and regression slopes.
about=QGIS port of the University of Illinois VB6/MapWinGIS "Yield Statistics" program (prjYields). Choose two point layers (e.g. yield-monitor shapefiles) and a yield column for each. Reports descriptive, non-parametric and robust statistics, Jarque-Bera normality tests and Gaussian and empirical exceedance values, and tests whether the two fields have equal means, medians, variances and histograms. Optional least-squares and reweighted (least-median-of-squares) regression of yield against another column, with slope tests. Both layers can be coloured by yield with a graduated renderer. Uses only QGIS (numpy; matplotlib for the plots).
