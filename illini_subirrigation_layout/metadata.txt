name=Illini Subirrigation Layout
qgisMinimumVersion=3.34
description=Zonal subirrigation (controlled drainage) tile layout from a DEM, field boundary and outlet: every drainage subsystem is contained within its own elevation zone and held by its own control structure.
about=Cuts the field into zones (elevation bands one interval high, or zone polygons you supply, e.g. from the Water Management Zones plugin) and lays out a tile system in which each zone is one subsystem. Laterals stay inside their zone, keep a setback from every zone boundary and join only their own zone's collectors; a zone no collector reaches gets its own submain with an unperforated connector to a lower zone. Control structures are placed wherever a collector carries water out of a zone, with board (stop-log) elevations that hold the managed water table a set depth below each zone's low ground. Reports, per zone, the share of land with the water table within the control depth, the share of tile below the board, and the water supply rate. Tile burying with Routine J, Minimum cover, Fewest grade breaks, Least cost, or Level within zones (the tile of each zone near level below its board). The layout, tile burying and pipe sizing (Routine K, with the Strahler order of Routine G) are those of the Illini Subsurface Drain Layout plugin, ported from the Illini Drainage Tools by Anamelechi Falasy and Richard Cooke, University of Illinois Urbana-Champaign (GPL v3). Uses only QGIS, GDAL and numpy.

changelog=0.5.1 - Sizing: no pipe is now sized smaller than a pipe draining into it (a short steep link near the outlet had sized smaller than the flatter pipe above); such links are flagged 'sized up to N in to match the pipe above'. Layout: a lateral whose trace stopped short on flat or rough ground, with no collector near, is carried on downhill to a collector or to a lateral that reaches one (flagged 'carried downhill'), and no lateral tees into one that does not drain; the warning now counts the laterals draining into an unconnected one.
    0.5.0 - New optional No-tile zones input: polygons (ponds, waterways, building sites, wetlands) cut out of the field like holes in the boundary; laterals keep the boundary setback from them, collectors go around them, and solid connectors are routed around them instead of across. New option Mains and submains may cross the no-tile zones (for grassed waterways): laterals stay out of the zones, the main and the solid connectors may run across them.
    0.4.0 - New Report units setting: the layout report (with its zone and structure tables) and the summary in the log in acres, feet and gpm (the default) or hectares, metres and L/s.
    0.3.0 - New lateral shape 8-point (Illini Subsurface Drain Layout engine 0.21.0): laterals bend with the terrain, every leg in one of the 8 compass directions.
    0.2.0 - Built on the Illini Subsurface Drain Layout engine 0.20.0: one drainage coefficient for every line (default 0.375 inch/day) with D_COEFF, DRAIN_SPACE and PIPE_TYPE fields; burying methods Routine J, Minimum cover, Fewest grade breaks and Least cost, with the shortest run and largest change of grade at a break; Bury from the outlet invert; layout problems only in flag. New burying method Level within zones: the tile of each zone near level and at or below the zone's board, with few grade breaks, so all of it can take water in from the structure.
    0.1.0 - First release. Zonal subirrigation layout built on the Illini Subsurface Drain Layout engine (0.15.1): elevation-band or supplied zones, laterals contained within their zone, zone submains and connectors, control structures at every zone exit with board elevations, per-zone water-table control, tile below the board and water supply, a Subirrigation zones layer, a Control structures layer and an optional Water-table status raster.
