about=The worldwide, metric companion to SSURGO drainage soil map. Give it a field boundary, or trace one on the map. It downloads ISRIC SoilGrids 2.0 (250 m) sand, clay and bulk density at six depths to 2 m, groups the cells into soil zones by USDA texture above and below the drains, estimates Ksat with Rosetta 3, and gives each zone a Hooghoudt drain spacing and an inverse Hooghoudt subirrigation spacing, to the nearest metre. SoilGrids values are 250 m predictions, not mapped soils, so treat the results as a first estimate. Requires an internet connection.
