name=Depressions and Laylines
qgisMinimumVersion=3.22
description=Depressions and laylines (flow channels) in a field from a LiDAR DEM, for subsurface drainage design.
about=For subsurface (tile) drainage design. From a field's bare-earth DEM (for example from the LiDAR Field DEM plugin):
    Depressions: depth = filled DEM - unfilled DEM; cells deeper than a set depth (default 0.75 ft) inside the field are mapped as polygons with area, maximum and mean depth, storage volume and pond level - candidate spots for surface inlets or extra laterals.
    Laylines: the flow channels within the field, routed downhill over the filled DEM, with Strahler order and contributing area - the natural routes for mains and submains.
    Contours: optional contour lines of the filled DEM (default 1 ft), with labeled index contours.
    Sinks are filled with the Wang & Liu (2006) method when no filled DEM is given. Pure Python + NumPy; needs no SAGA or GRASS.
version=0.3.0

changelog=0.3.0 - DEM color scheme choice: Hypsometric green-yellow-brown (default), Greens or Tan/brown, stretched over the field (2nd-98th percentile), with an optional multiplied hillshade (on by default). Darker contours.
    0.2.2 - Colors the DEM with the Greens color ramp (option, on by default).
    0.2.1 - Contours are drawn from the filled DEM.
    0.2.0 - Optional contours output (default 1 ft interval, labeled index contour every 5th), clipped to the field.
    0.1.0 - First release as a separate plugin (the tool started in LiDAR Field DEM 0.4.0): depressions deeper than 0.75 ft from filled - unfilled DEM; laylines = flow channels within the field.
