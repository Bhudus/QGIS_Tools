name=Subsurface Drainage Layout
qgisMinimumVersion=3.34
description=Automated subsurface (tile) drainage layout from a DEM, field boundary, outlet and lateral spacing.
about=Lays out mains, submains and evenly spaced laterals running near parallel or near perpendicular to the field contours, in parallel/gridiron or herringbone patterns, with straight or contour-following laterals. Designs pipe inverts at minimum cover and grade and flags pipes that are too deep, need long connectors or have no collector downstream. Runs from the Processing Toolbox or the plugin toolbar; uses only QGIS, GDAL and numpy.
