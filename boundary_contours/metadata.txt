qgisMinimumVersion=3.16
qgisMaximumVersion=4.99
supportsQt6=True
description=Contour lines from a DEM, optionally sink-filled and smoothed, limited to a boundary polygon, with index contours.
about=Works like Raster > Extraction > Contour, with more options. It can process only the part of the DEM inside a boundary polygon layer and clip the contours to it. It can contour a depression-filled DEM, with pits filled flat to their spill level, using a built-in Priority-Flood fill or SAGA Wang & Liu. It can smooth the DEM (Gaussian) and/or the lines (Chaikin). It flags every Nth contour as an index contour (INDEX/TYPE fields), with optional automatic styling and labels. It remembers the last-run settings for each project. Available in the Processing Toolbox and under Raster > Tile Drainage. Uses only QGIS, GDAL and numpy.
