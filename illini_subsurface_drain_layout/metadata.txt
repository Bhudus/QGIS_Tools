name=Illini Subsurface Drain Layout
qgisMinimumVersion=3.34
description=Automated subsurface (tile) drainage layout and tile burying from a DEM, field boundary, outlet and lateral spacing.
about=Lays out mains, submains and evenly spaced laterals running near parallel or near perpendicular to the field contours, in parallel/gridiron or herringbone patterns, with straight or contour-following laterals, as one connected network. Buries the tile with the scheme of Routine J (Tile Burying System) of the Illini Drainage Tools: from the outlet upstream, toward a target depth band, within minimum and maximum slopes and absolute depth limits, with optional constant slope, outlet offset and outlet lowering for low ground. Writes the Routine J fields (ElevDepth1/2, BuryDepth1/2, InSlope, OutSlope, BURY_CHECK) and a line-ID layer for the Illini Drainage Tools. Uses only QGIS, GDAL and numpy. The burying scheme is ported from the Illini Drainage Tools by Anamelechi Falasy and Richard Cooke, University of Illinois Urbana-Champaign (GPL v3).
